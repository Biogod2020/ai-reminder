# Weekly Review

Use `secretary_task(action="list", status="active")`, `secretary_brief(range="week")`, and `secretary_feedback(action="stats", days=30)`.

Report:

1. deadlines likely to miss without additional capacity;
2. planned versus completed work when explicit feedback exists;
3. contexts whose duration estimates are systematically high or low;
4. hours with enough feedback to suggest a productivity pattern;
5. at most three concrete changes for next week.

Distinguish evidence from hypothesis. Sparse feedback is not a reliable habit. Never write a new timezone, work window, energy profile, or autonomy mode without showing the proposed value and receiving confirmation.

The review should reduce future planning error, not produce a long retrospective narrative.
