# Authoritative Sources for SOTA Research

## Academic Literature
- **arXiv.org**: The primary repository for preprints in physics, computer science, and AI.
- **Google Scholar**: Comprehensive academic search.
- **ACL Anthology**: For NLP and computational linguistics.
- **IEEE Xplore**: For engineering and computer science.

## High-Signal Aggregators
- **Papers With Code**: (paperswithcode.com) - SOTA benchmarks and leaderboards.
- **Hugging Face**: (huggingface.co/papers) - Trending research.
- **LabML.ai**: (labml.ai) - Annotated deep learning papers.
