# Research Summary: SOTA Agentic AI & Multimodal Reasoning

## Models & Frameworks
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite:** SOTA for high-volume, low-latency agentic tasks (86.9% GPQA Diamond).
- **LangGraph / PydanticAI:** State-of-the-art orchestration for multi-agent loops.

## Reasoning & Alignment
- **UA2 Principle (Unified Alignment):** Aligning agents with human intent, environment, and self-constraints.
- **SRICE Framework (2025):** Uncertainty Quantification for tool use and clarification loops.
- **Agency Preservation:** Ensuring AI protects user autonomy rather than manipulating intent.
