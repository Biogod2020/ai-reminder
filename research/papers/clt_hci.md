# Research Summary: Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) & HCI

## Foundational Books
- **Cognitive Load Theory (John Sweller et al., 2011)**
- **User and Task Analysis for Interface Design (Hackos & Redish, 1998)**

## Key Papers
- **Hollender et al. (2010):** Integrating CLT and HCI.
- **Kosch et al. (2023):** Measuring Cognitive Workload in HCI.

## Core Principles for Notion-Soul-Agent
- **Intrinsic vs. Extraneous Load:** Minimize interface noise to focus on task execution.
- **Cognitive Offloading:** Use AI to handle scheduling and reminders.
